# Cut vs Continuation Pacing Reference

## Joining Logic Patterns
- **Action Match (`CUT`)**: Cut cleanly just as the hand reaches or sets down the prop, carrying momentum into the tighter framing.
- **Eyeline & Reaction Breath**: After a line ends, allow at least a short silent reaction beat before cutting away or concluding the segment so speech does not feel clipped.
- **Continuous Follow (`last_frame`)**: Keep motion speed and screen direction uniform across the boundary so the downstream continuation segment picks up smoothly without a visible jump.
