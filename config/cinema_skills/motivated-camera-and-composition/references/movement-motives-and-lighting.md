# Movement Motives & Lighting Continuity Reference

## Motivated Movement Patterns
- **Static Observation**: "Static tripod medium shot at eye level; subject anchored on screen left third, doorway visible in soft background depth."
- **Reveal Push-In**: "Motivated by the subject noticing the unsealed envelope, a slow small push-in from medium shot to medium close-up, stopping cleanly before her line."
- **Lateral Follow**: "Gentle small lateral track matching the performer's walk along the corridor wall, maintaining constant profile distance."

## Lighting & Identity Legibility
- Maintain consistent key-light side (e.g., soft cool window key from screen left, warm practical tungsten fill on screen right) across shots in the same room.
- Avoid heavy face-obscuring silhouettes or extreme strobe contrast unless explicitly required by the frozen Style Bible, so character facial likeness remains stable across frames.
