# Two-Person Blocking & Internal Cut Staging

## Keeping Two Performers Distinct & Grounded
- Anchor one performer on screen left and the other on screen right (or foreground vs midground depth) and preserve that side across `last_frame` boundaries and reaction cuts.
- Describe who acts first and who reacts second so subject references stay unambiguous and faces never swap.
- Avoid simultaneous crossed arms or overlapping silhouettes during prop handoffs; let one hand extend clearly into readable negative space.

## Internal Cut Transitions
- When `editorial_timing_plan` folds beats into `cuts[]`, keep the opening segment focused on establishing the action and let each `cuts[].visual_description` show the specific new angle, insert, or reaction beat at `start_s` without repeating the entire opening summary.
