# Prop & Posture Anchoring Reference

## Anti-Pattern vs Filmable Beat
- **Avoid (unfilmable mental summary)**: "Lin feels anxious and remembers the promise she made years ago."
- **Prefer (three-phase physical beat)**: "Lin sits upright on the wooden bench with both feet flat on the stone floor, fingers pressing the edge of the folded letter until her knuckles whiten; she exhales slowly and turns her chin toward the half-open doorway."

## Prop Interaction Checklist
- Specify **which hand** holds, sets down, or slides a core prop (`the sealed envelope`, `the brass key`, `the porcelain cup`).
- Keep ordinary background dressing in prose description rather than promoting incidental items into required `asset_ids`.
- When a character enters or exits a chair/doorway, explicitly state weight transfer and contact with the floor or frame so video generation avoids floating or ground penetration.
