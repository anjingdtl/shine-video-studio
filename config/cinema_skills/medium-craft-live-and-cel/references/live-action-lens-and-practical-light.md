# Live-Action Lens & Practical Light Craft

## Optical Depth & Physical Grounding
- Specify realistic lens separation (for example, medium focal perspective keeping natural facial proportions and gentle background falloff) and real surface contact (shoe soles on wet cobblestones, forearm resting on oak table).
- Ground lighting in visible practical or environmental sources (overcast window light, warm desk lamp, rain-slick lantern bounce) with natural skin tones and subtle physical shadow gradients.
