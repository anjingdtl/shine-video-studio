# 2D Cel Animation Pose & Silhouette Craft

## Readable Key Poses & Cel Light
- Stage gestures against uncluttered negative space in the painted background so dark/colored line-art contours stay crisp during motion.
- Use clear anticipation before major movement (a slight weight shift or shoulder draw-back before stepping forward) and hold a clean settle pose at the segment end.
- Describe shadows as clean, hard-edged two-tone cel shapes cast consistently from one scene light source, keeping costume folds simple and consistent with the character sheet.
